# DAN floor patch: the punish arm was a build artefact, the reward arm is not (2026-09-21)

The reward-path plan's hypothesis was that the `>=5` synapse floor in
`brain_gpu.npz` deletes the sugar -> PAM path, exactly as the floor-adjacent APL
in-degree bug deleted APL's inputs. The patch was built, diffed, and swept at 10
seeds. It repairs the punish arm and does nothing at all for reward.

Scripts: `regime/patch_dan_floor.py` (build + diff), `regime/test_dan_floor.py`
(subset / byte-identity test), `learn/us_path_sweep.py` (10-seed sweep).
Matrices: `data/brain_gpu.npz` (default), `data/brain_gpu_danfloor1.npz` (patched).
Raw: `results/us_path_sweep_base.json`, `results/us_path_sweep_danfloor1.json`,
`results/us_path_sweep_base_mn.json`.

Command lines:

```
.venv/Scripts/python.exe regime/patch_dan_floor.py
.venv/Scripts/python.exe regime/test_dan_floor.py
.venv/Scripts/python.exe learn/us_path_sweep.py --seeds 10 --tag base
FLYCHESS_BRAIN=data/brain_gpu_danfloor1.npz .venv/Scripts/python.exe learn/us_path_sweep.py --seeds 10 --tag danfloor1
.venv/Scripts/python.exe learn/us_path_sweep.py --seeds 3 --tag base_mn --mn subclass:proboscis_motor_neuron
```

Matrix selection is via the **`FLYCHESS_BRAIN` env var**, read at import by
`gpu_sim` and `learn/plastic`; there is no `--npz` flag. `test_dan_floor.py` is a
plain script with top-level assertions and prints, not a pytest module - pytest
is not installed in this venv.

## 1. The floor does delete the PAM inputs, at the predicted scale

From the raw `data/ext/proofread_connections_783.feather` (16.85M rows ->
15,090,883 neuron pairs; 1,117 rows dropped because the root id is not in the
annotations):

| population | pairs floor>=5 | pairs floor>=1 | in-deg 5 -> 1 | syn/neuron 5 -> 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PAM (307) | 2,231 | 84,375 | 7.3 -> 274.8 | 51.6 -> 413.7 |
| PPL1 (16) | 3,149 | 25,228 | 196.8 -> 1576.8 | 2309.8 -> 4804.0 |

Kept fractions under the floor, for reference: MBON 72%, KC 49%, PPL1 48%,
**PAM 12.5%**. PAM is the outlier by a factor of four.

## 2. The patched matrix is the same brain plus DAN inputs, verified

`data/brain_gpu_danfloor1.npz`: nnz 2,700,429 -> 2,804,652 (5,380 removed,
109,603 added; of the additions, KC->PAM is 62,337 pairs / 85,527 syn).

- Every non-target column is **byte-identical** to the default matrix.
- Subset check proves the shipped npz is exactly the raw `>=5` counts, so the
  25%-inflated-weight history does not repeat.
- Hop-2 sugar-GRN-target -> PAM pairs: 2 -> 28.
- Sugar GRNs reach **306 of 307** PAM within 3 hops on the patched matrix.
- `regime/test_dan_floor.py` passes; `GpuSim` constructs on CUDA against it.
- **`NORM_TOTAL_TARGET = 0.0` (off) during every run in this doc** (`gpu_sim.py`
  l.153 default). `regime/measure.py` and `regime/test_gap.py` l.174 set it to
  29.15, and under normalisation the PAM rows on the patched matrix are rescaled
  about 8x relative to default, because the per-postsynaptic factor divides out
  the in-degree this patch just restored. Any number measured under
  normalisation is **not** comparable to this doc.
- `min_syn` stays 5 in the patched npz even though 104,223 of the 109,603 target
  edges are below it; the `dan_floor` / `dan_floor_targets` keys carry the truth.
  Nothing consumes `min_syn` for logic today, so this is a provenance wart, not
  a bug - but do not filter on it.
- `learn/plastic.py` offsets cache is now keyed by nnz
  (`data/plastic_offsets_<nnz>.npz`) so the two matrices cannot cross-load.

## 3. The 10-seed sweep

`learn/us_path_sweep.py`, 10 seeds, misery song plus 150 Hz on 65 bitter / 129
sugar GRNs, threshold = baseline + 1.0 Hz, exactly `check_us_path`.

Default matrix (`n_edges` 2,700,429), 94.9 s:

| arm | measure | mean Hz | SD | range | seeds over threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| punish | PPL1 under bitter | 1.000 | 0.451 | 0.278-1.944 | 4/10 |
| reward | PAM under sugar | 0.024 | 0.049 | 0.000-0.141 | 0/10 |
| reward | PAM baseline | 0.021 | 0.043 | 0.000-0.130 | - |
| punish | PPL1 baseline | 0.056 | 0.111 | 0.000-0.278 | - |

Decision `dan` 10/10. Regime: kc_active 0.0500 (base / bitter / sugar alike)
**(authored, invariant: fingerprint graft)**; central_active 0.0176 / 0.0326 /
0.0276.

Patched matrix (`n_edges` 2,804,652, dan_floor 1), 66.7 s:

| arm | measure | mean Hz | SD | range | seeds over threshold |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| punish | PPL1 under bitter | 6.750 | 2.019 | 3.611-10.278 | **9/10** |
| reward | PAM under sugar | 0.097 | 0.099 | 0.000-0.228 | **0/10** |
| reward | PAM baseline | 0.119 | 0.115 | 0.000-0.326 | - |
| punish | PPL1 baseline | 2.250 | 1.926 | 0.000-5.556 | - |

Decision `dan` 10/10. Regime: kc_active 0.0500 at all three conditions
**(authored, invariant: fingerprint graft)**; central_active 0.0242 / 0.0340 /
0.0317.

`kc_active` is not a measurement here. `learn/fingerprint.py` l.57-65 grafts the
CS by driving exactly `SPARSITY * n_KC` Kenyon cells at `KC_HZ` = 50 Hz on every
trial, so `(rates[KC] > 1).mean()` is pinned at 0.0500 whatever the matrix or the
US does. **`central_active` (0.0176 -> 0.0242, delta 0.0066) is the only real
regime criterion this sweep tested.** A genuine KC-fraction check needs an
odour-driven paradigm, not the song graft.

## 4. MN9 / proboscis: the GRN drive itself is fine

`MN9` is **not** in the v783 annotations: zero hits on any column, no
`hemibrain_type` starting with `MN`, and the MN-named `brain_motor_neuron` types
are MN10 x3, MNx01 x3, MNx03 x2. The usable handle is
`cell_sub_class == proboscis_motor_neuron`, 24 neurons (CB0720, CB0762, CB0783,
CB0789, CB0845, CB0858, CB0861, CB0871, CB0911). For context,
`brain_motor_neuron` totals 105 in 45 types: ingestion 28, neck 25, proboscis
24, antennal 10, crop 8, eye 4, haustellum 4, salivary 2.

On the **default (unpatched)** matrix, 3 seeds:

| population | under sugar | baseline |
|---|---|---|
| proboscis MNs | **37.269 Hz** (SD 0.720, 36.25-37.78) | 0.000 Hz |

150 Hz sugar drive is real and propagates several synapses to the correct motor
output; Shiu 2024's PER result reproduces qualitatively. The failure is specific
to the sugar -> PAM branch, and input in-degree is not what fixes it.

## Verdict

**FAIL** against the plan stop condition (reward >= 8/10 AND punish >= 8/10 AND
|d kc_active| <= 0.01 AND |d central_active| <= 0.01): reward is 0/10. Punish
passes at 9/10, kc_active moves 0.0000 - but that criterion is vacuous, because
kc_active is authored, invariant under the fingerprint graft (see above), so
**`central_active`, which moves 0.0066 (0.0176 -> 0.0242), is the only real
regime criterion that was tested.** Nothing was tuned after the FAIL. The floor patch repairs the punish arm (PPL1 1.00 -> 6.75
Hz, though its baseline also rises 0.056 -> 2.25 Hz with SD 1.93, so it is a
larger rise on a noisier floor) and does nothing for reward: PAM under sugar is
0.097 Hz against a baseline of 0.119 Hz, i.e. **below** baseline, after PAM
in-degree went 7.3 -> 274.8. Meanwhile the same sugar drive puts proboscis motor
neurons at 37 Hz from silence on the unpatched matrix. Restoring input count is
not the missing piece.

## How to apply

- Do not propose input in-degree, the synapse floor, or GRN drive strength as
  the reward fix. All three are measured out: the floor is restored, 306/307 PAM
  are within 3 hops, and the drive reaches motor neurons at 37 Hz.
- Do not call sugar -> PAM "biology" on the strength of the old floor-5 numbers;
  the anatomy-gap explanation is now tested and dead. Hunger / octopamine state
  gating is still an open, untested explanation, not a conclusion.
- The patch is **not** adopted. If it is ever adopted for the punish arm alone,
  weigh the PPL1 baseline rise (0.056 -> 2.25 Hz, SD 1.93) as a regime cost: the
  punish signal-to-noise improves less than the mean suggests.
- Phase 4 (reward arm learning) is blocked. Every learning result stays
  punishment-only with the US injected at the DANs.

### Next lever (open hypothesis, not run)

**Signed-path analysis, sugar GRN -> PAM on the patched matrix.** Which
intermediate cell types carry the hop-2 / hop-3 paths; what fraction of the
restored PAM input is inhibitory (GABA / glut presynaptic); and whether the
SMP/CRE "none"-class intermediates are themselves driven or inhibited under
sugar drive. PAM sitting *below* baseline under sugar is the tell: the restored
input may be net inhibitory.

Second candidate: PAM input at floor 1 is dominated by KC->PAM (85.5k syn),
which is silent without odour, so most of the restored in-degree cannot carry a
taste signal at all.
